# Resolution quality gate

1. Cite observations with source/evidence IDs.
2. Label inferred causes and unverified assumptions.
3. Record confidence and missing evidence.
4. Verify KB product/version applicability.
5. Classify actions as read-only, disruptive, or risky.
6. Require explicit human approval and rollback for disruptive/risky actions.
7. Define success criteria and a monitoring window.
8. Use only the most relevant official references in customer output.
